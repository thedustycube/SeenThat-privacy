SeenThat – Account and Data Deletion Request

At SeenThat, we respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal data.

1. How to Delete Your Account In-App

If you have the app installed, you can delete your account immediately:

  • Open SeenThat and log in.
  • Go to Settings (or Profile).
  • Tap Delete Account and confirm.

2. How to Request Deletion via Web / Email

If you uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can submit a manual deletion request:

  • Send an email to: support@yourdomain.com (or your support email)
  • Subject line: SeenThat Account Deletion Request
  • Include the registered email address tied to your SeenThat account.

Requests are processed within 7 business days.

3. Data Deletion and Retention Policy

  • Data Deleted: Your user profile, authentication credentials, custom watchlists, episode viewing progress, ratings, and stored personal preferences will be permanently purged from our servers.
  • Data Retained: We do not retain any identifiable personal records after account deletion.
